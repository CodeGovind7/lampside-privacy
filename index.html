Lampside privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Lampside is a book and document reader. It has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else. We have no server. Everything below happens on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your library: the list of your books and documents, where you are in each, and whether a file is a book or a document.
  • Your quotes, notes and bookmarks.
  • Your reading habit: time read, daily goal, streak and reminders.
  • Your settings.

None of this leaves your device unless you choose one of the options under "When your data leaves your device".

Access to your files

You decide where Lampside may look for books and documents. You can choose folders, and Lampside then sees only what is in those folders. Where the app offers it, you can instead let it look through all files on the device, so that it finds your books wherever they are kept. You can also open files one at a time with "Open a file", without giving access to any folder.

Lampside reads files of the types it can open (EPUB, PDF, FB2, MOBI, AZW3, DOCX, ODT, RTF, TXT, Markdown, HTML and CBZ). It deletes or renames a file only when you ask it to. It does not upload your files. For a book in a folder you chose, it may keep its own copy inside the app so that the book can be opened; that copy is removed when the book leaves the library or the app is uninstalled.

Notifications

If you turn on the reading reminder, Lampside shows a notification at the time you set, and a weekly summary. They are created on your device.

When your data leaves your device

Only when you ask for it:

  • Ask AI and Define. The text you selected and the question you chose are handed to the app you pick (for example an AI assistant or a dictionary). What that app does with it is covered by that app's own privacy policy. Lampside sends nothing by itself.
  • Sharing. A quote, a quote picture, exported highlights or a file is handed to the app you choose in the share sheet.
  • Sync between devices. If you set it up, Lampside keeps one file in a storage location you choose (for example your own cloud drive). It holds your reading positions, lists, quotes and notes, reading time, collections and settings. It is stored by your storage provider under your own account; we never receive it.
  • Backups. A backup file you create, or the weekly backup you turn on, is saved where you choose. If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may also keep a copy of Lampside's library data and settings in your Google account, so they return when you set up a new phone. Google handles that copy under its privacy policy.

Children

Lampside is a general reading tool. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app's listing, with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the developer contact address shown on Lampside's Google Play page.
